Multi-Business Website Strategy & Modernization Analysis
Executive Overview
You operate three distinct businesses, each with its own market, customers, and revenue model:
  • Commercial Renovations (B2B – Buffalo, NY)
  • Bath-to-Shower Conversions & Installations (B2C – Buffalo, NY)
  • Linen Rental for Short-Term Rentals (B2B – Miami & Orlando, FL)
Although they share ownership, these businesses should not share a single digital strategy.
Each business currently relies on a basic or outdated WordPress setup that no longer aligns with its growth potential. This is preventing you from getting potential clients.
This analysis outlines:
  • What is broken or limiting growth
  • Where the real opportunities are
  • Why a modern rebuild is justified
  • A realistic, risk-controlled implementation plan
Core Strategic Insight
This is not a "new website" situation.
It is a business enablement problem:
  • buffreno.com shows residential work but needs to portray commercial expertise
  • bathshowerunlimited.com cannot transact online at all
  • linenbnb.com lacks visibility and trust in competitive Miami and Orlando markets
Incremental WordPress updates would not solve these structural issues.

Business #3: linenbnb.com
Linen Rental – Miami & Orlando, FL
Current Situation
  • Outdated WordPress site
  • Minimal content
  • Weak trust signals
  • Poor local SEO
  • Low visibility in competitive markets
Value Proposition Gap
The current website fails to communicate why linen rental is superior to traditional cleaning services:
Linen Rental Model Advantages:
  • Cost Efficiency – Predictable monthly expense vs. per-turnover cleaning charges
  • Operational Simplicity – Eliminate laundry logistics, storage, and management overhead
  • Consistency & Quality – Hotel-grade linens every time, not worn-out personal inventory
  • Time Savings – Focus on hosting and guest experience, not laundry schedules
  • Scalability – Easily expand to multiple properties without operational complexity
  • Professional Standards – Hospitality-quality presentation improves guest satisfaction and reviews
The new website must clearly convey these advantages to convert Airbnb and vacation rental hosts.
SnowPeak Recommendation
A modern, hospitality-focused redesign optimized for local visibility and trust.
What This Site Should Do:
  • Communicate value proposition – Clearly explain why linen rental beats cleaning services
  • Highlight cost-efficiency – Show predictable pricing model vs. per-turnover expenses
  • Emphasize operational benefits – No laundry, no storage, no hassle
  • Showcase quality standards – Hotel-grade linens, professional presentation
  • Clearly explain services and coverage areas
  • Establish credibility quickly
  • Target Miami and Orlando search terms with SEO
  • Convert website visits into inquiries
  • Perform well on mobile
Timeline: 2–3 weeks | Investment: $1,500 CAD
