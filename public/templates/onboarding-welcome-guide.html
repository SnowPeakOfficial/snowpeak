Frequently Asked Questions

How do I provide feedback on designs or features?

We welcome your feedback! You can provide input via email, scheduled calls, or comments in our project portal. We'll incorporate your feedback during designated revision rounds.

What if I need changes after the project launches?

We offer a 30-day warranty for bug fixes after launch. For new features or changes, we offer maintenance packages starting at $300/month or hourly rates for one-off updates.

How do revision rounds work?

Your package includes specified revision rounds. We'll present work at key milestones, gather your feedback, and make adjustments. Additional revisions beyond your package can be added as needed.

What happens if the timeline needs to change?

We'll communicate proactively about any timeline adjustments. Most changes occur due to scope additions or delayed client feedback. We'll work together to find solutions that work for both parties.

How is payment structured?

Typically: 50% deposit to begin, 25% at midpoint milestone, and 25% upon completion. We accept bank transfer, credit card, or other arrangements discussed during onboarding.

Will I own the code and designs?

Yes! Upon final payment, you receive full ownership of all code, designs, and assets created for your project. We provide everything needed for future maintenance.
