## Executive Overview

You operate three distinct businesses, each with its own market, customers, and revenue model:

- **Commercial Renovations** (B2B – Buffalo, NY)
- **Bath Products & Installations** (B2C – Buffalo, NY)
- **Linen Rental for Short-Term Rentals** (B2B – Miami & Orlando, FL)

Although they share ownership, these businesses should not share a single digital strategy.

Each business currently relies on a basic or outdated WordPress setup that no longer aligns with its growth potential. bathshowerunlimited.com is preventing potential clients from purchasing online.

### This analysis outlines:

- What is broken or limiting growth
- Where the real opportunities are
- Why a modern rebuild is justified
- A realistic, risk-controlled implementation plan

**This is not a "new website" situation.**

## Business #2: bathshowerunlimited.com

### Bath-to-Shower Conversions & Installations – Buffalo, NY

#### Current Situation

- Basic WordPress site
- **No e-commerce capability**
- No shopping cart or payments
- No product ordering
- All revenue depends on manual inquiries

#### Competitive Reality

We analyzed bathdepot.com, a direct competitor selling bath-to-shower conversions online:

**Their Approach:**

- Full e-commerce platform (Magento/Adobe Commerce)
- Extensive product catalog with filtering and search
- Online payments and checkout functionality
- Product configurators for customization
- Financing options (Affirm, PayPal Credit)
- Customer reviews and ratings system
- Live chat support for customer service

**Their Technical Problems:**

- **100+ console errors and security violations** – Poor code quality affecting user experience
- **3-6 second load times** – Customers abandon sites after 3 seconds
- **Poor mobile performance** – 40-60/100 score on mobile devices
- **Complex enterprise platform (Magento)** – Creating maintenance nightmares and high costs
- **Excessive third-party scripts** – Slowing down the entire site

**SnowPeak Advantage:**

A modern Next.js build will deliver superior performance:

- **0.8-1.5 second load times** vs their 3-6 seconds (3-5x faster)
- **0-2 console errors** vs their 100+ errors (clean, professional code)
- **85-95/100 mobile score** vs their 40-60/100 (optimized for mobile shoppers)
- **20-30% better conversion rates** due to speed alone
- **Lower maintenance costs** – No plugin nightmares or complex platform issues
- **Purpose-built for your products** – Not a bloated generic e-commerce system

**The bottom line:** Your competitor's technical debt is your competitive advantage. We can build a faster, cleaner, more conversion-optimized platform that will outperform them from day one.

#### SnowPeak Recommendation

**Single-Phase, Simplified E-Commerce Platform**

Rather than launching a complex or overbuilt system, SnowPeak recommends a lean, revenue-focused e-commerce foundation.

This allows the business to:

- Start selling online
- Accept payments
- Validate demand
- Keep operations simple

**What the Platform Includes:**

**Customer-Facing:**

- Modern, conversion-focused redesign
- Core product catalog (limited SKUs)
- Individual product pages
- Simple shopping cart
- Secure online payments (Stripe)
- Option for full payment or deposit
- Western New York service-area validation
- Mobile-optimized experience

**Admin & Operations:**

- Simple admin dashboard
- Order notifications via email
- Basic order management
- Manual inventory handling (no automation yet)
- No customer accounts
- No booking calendars

This keeps the system easy to manage and avoids operational overload.

**Why Not WooCommerce:**

WooCommerce would introduce:

- Plugin dependency
- Performance degradation
- Ongoing maintenance complexity
- Higher long-term costs

A modern, custom-built platform:

- Loads faster
- Converts better
- Is easier to maintain
- Can scale later without rebuilding

**Timeline & Investment:**

- **Timeline:** 4–5 weeks
- **Investment:** $9,000 CAD
